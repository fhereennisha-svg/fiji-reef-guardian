Environmental JavaScript Game

Fiji Reef Guardian

Guide a sea turtle through three marine habitats, remove ocean waste and avoid dangerous fishing equipment.

Select a difficulty and press Space or Start.

How to play

  • Select a difficulty using 1, 2 or 3.
  • Press Space or the Start button to begin.
  • On a computer, move using the arrow keys.
  • On a phone, move using the touch controls.
  • Collect plastic bottles, cans, bags, ropes and fishing nets.
  • Avoid boats and dangerous fishing hooks.
  • Collect bubble shields for protection.
  • Press N or the action button after completing an area.
  • Press R or the action button after winning or losing.

Marine areas

  1. Coral Lagoon
  2. Mangrove Channel
  3. Deep Ocean Reef

Environmental mission

Protect marine animals by collecting waste and avoiding abandoned or active fishing equipment.

Created as a JavaScript and p5.js learning project.
